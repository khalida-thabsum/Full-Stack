Rayalaseema Road Trip
Route Stops Tips Book

Five days, four districts, one long drive through the Deccan

Caves, rock gardens, a hill temple and a 16th-century carved pavilion, on a loop you can start from Kurnool or Tirupati.

The route

  1. Day 1Kurnool to Orvakal Rock Garden
  2. Day 2Belum Caves
  3. Day 3Lepakshi
  4. Day 4Penukonda Fort
  5. Day 5Tirumala

Stops

Travel tips

When to go

November to February. Summer days cross 40°C.

Getting around

A self-drive car or a hired cab; bus links between stops are slow.

What to carry

Water, a cap, and slip-on footwear for temples.
